==============================================================================
 FOUR DISTINCT DEGRADATION BEHAVIOURS
==============================================================================

1. THE RUN FINISHES. Exit 0, a complete briefing. One dead source does not cost
   you the other three. Aborting would throw away the audit, quality and news
   findings, which are unaffected by a logistics outage.

2. THE OUTAGE IS STATED AT THE TOP, NOT INFERRED:
       > Sources unavailable: logistics unavailable (timeout)
   A reader is told what they are NOT seeing, which is the difference between a
   partial briefing and a misleading one.

3. 'UNREACHABLE' IS NOT 'NOTHING TO REPORT'. late_shipment_count drops into
   Incomplete carrying its cause:
       ### late_shipment_count  _[missing source: timeout reading logistics]_
   Compare the other Incomplete entry, which failed for a different reason:
       ### production_capacity_utilization  _[missing source: no source reported this metric]_
   Same section, different annotation. 'We could not ask' and 'we asked and nobody
   knew' are different operational problems: the first is retryable, the second is a
   coverage gap that needs a new source. Collapsing them into one 'missing' state
   would lose the only information that tells you which action to take.

4. CORROBORATION IS LOST, NOT FAKED. average_lead_time_days keeps its 12.0-day
   value but is demoted from '[corroborated across 2 sources]' to '[single source
   only]'. The number did not change; the CONFIDENCE IN IT did, and the briefing
   says so.

==============================================================================
 ⚠️  THE FINDING THAT MATTERS MOST: A CONFLICT CAN DISAPPEAR
==============================================================================

In the normal run, on_time_delivery_rate is the headline risk:

    ## Contested
    ### on_time_delivery_rate  _[2 sources, conflicting]_  ⚠️ ESCALATE
    - escalation: high-impact metric is contested across sources
        - 95.0 percent — supplier_audit (as of 2026-04-10)
        - 78.0 percent — logistics  (as of 2026-04-05)

In the timeout run it appears here instead:

    ## Well-Established
    ### on_time_delivery_rate  _[single source only]_
        - 95.0 percent — supplier_audit (as of 2026-04-10)

    ## Contested
    _none_

The disagreement did not get resolved. The dissenting source went offline, and the
surviving value is the SUPPLIER'S OWN SELF-REPORTED 95% — the optimistic one. The
pessimistic 78% came from our own logistics data. A metric that demanded escalation
is now filed under 'Well-Established', and the Contested section reads '_none_'.

Graceful degradation is not free. Losing a source does not merely reduce coverage;
it can convert a KNOWN DISAGREEMENT into FALSE CONFIDENCE, and it does so in the
reassuring direction. The '[single source only]' tag and the header outage banner
are the only things standing between this briefing and a reader who concludes
on-time delivery is fine.

Operational conclusion: source availability is not an infrastructure metric, it is a
DATA-QUALITY metric. It belongs on the same dashboard as the findings themselves,
and a metric that was Contested in the last run but is Well-Established in this one
because a source dropped out deserves an alert of its own.
